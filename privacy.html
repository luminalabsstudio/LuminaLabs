Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Sudoku Scapes, Life Logic, Trackly, and other mobile applications (collectively referred to as the "Applications") created by LuminaLabs ("Service Provider", "we", "us"). Data practices differ between our ad-supported games and Trackly, so each Application is described separately below.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Sudoku Scapes & Life Logic (ad-supported games)

These games are free to play and rely on advertisements to fund development. Data collection for these Applications is primarily handled by our third-party advertising partners.

Information You Provide Directly: We do not require an account to play. The only personal data we receive directly is what you voluntarily provide if you contact us for support (e.g. your email address).

Automatically Collected Information (Advertising & Analytics): To display advertisements and analyze game performance, our third-party partners (like Google AdMob) may collect and process:

  • Device Identifiers: Unique identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (GAID), used to show personalized ads and limit repeat ad views.
  • Coarse Location: Approximate location derived from your IP address, to keep ads relevant to your region.
  • Usage Data: Interactions with the game (session duration, crash logs, diagnostic information).

B. Trackly

Trackly is a TV show and movie tracking app. Unlike our games, Trackly currently has no advertisements, no user accounts, and no analytics or crash-tracking SDKs. Its data handling is as follows:

Data stored on your device: Trackly stores your library (tracked shows/movies, episode watch status, ratings, reviews), custom lists, profile display name/username/bio, and app settings locally on your device only, using standard app storage. This data is never transmitted to us — Trackly has no backend server. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The in-app "Export my data" feature creates a JSON file that is shared only through the method you choose.

Data sent over the network: To show title information, Trackly sends your search terms and title requests to The Movie Database (TMDB) (themoviedb.org). No personal profile data or library contents are sent — only the search queries and title IDs needed to fetch metadata, artwork and episode information. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB; see TMDB's own policy at themoviedb.org/privacy-policy.

Optional device permissions:

  • Calendar: only used if you tap "Add to calendar" for an episode; creates a single event on your device. Trackly does not read your existing calendar.
  • Notifications: used to schedule local episode reminders you opt into. Generated on-device; no push server is involved.
  • File access: used only when you choose to import or export a file, through the system file picker.

If future versions of Trackly add accounts, cloud sync, ads or analytics, this section will be updated accordingly before that version is released.

2. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services for advertising, analytics and content. These third parties have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information.

  • Google AdMob — used by Sudoku Scapes and Life Logic
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — used by Trackly, to provide show/movie data

3. Data Retention

We retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. For Sudoku Scapes and Life Logic, automatically collected information is managed by our third-party analytics and advertising services per their own retention policies. For Trackly, all library and profile data lives only on your device and is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Applications do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we delete it immediately. If you are a parent or guardian aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

5. Your Rights (Opt-Out)

Sudoku Scapes / Life Logic: You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app. You may reset your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID) to limit ad tracking.

Trackly: Since Trackly stores data only on your device, you can delete it at any time via Settings → "Clear library & lists" in the app, or by uninstalling the app.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
